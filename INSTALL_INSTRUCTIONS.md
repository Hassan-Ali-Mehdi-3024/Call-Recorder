# Instructions to bypass Google Play Protect

## The app is being blocked because it's not from Play Store

This is **COMPLETELY NORMAL** for apps you build yourself!

### Quick Fix (Choose ONE):

#### Method 1: Install Anyway ✅ (Easiest)
1. When you see "App blocked" message
2. Tap "Got it" 
3. Look for "More details" or "Install anyway" option
4. Tap it and install

#### Method 2: Disable Play Protect Temporarily
1. Open **Google Play Store**
2. Tap your **Profile icon** (top right)
3. Select **Play Protect**
4. Tap **Settings** ⚙️ (gear icon)
5. Turn OFF **"Scan apps with Play Protect"**
6. Install your APK
7. Turn Play Protect back ON after installation

#### Method 3: Allow Unknown Apps (One-time)
1. When blocked, tap "Settings"
2. Allow installation from this source
3. Go back and install

### Why is this happening?
- Your app is self-built, not from Play Store
- Google doesn't recognize the signing certificate
- This is a security feature, but YOUR app is safe (you built it!)

### Is it safe?
**YES!** You created this app yourself. You know exactly what's in it. Play Protect just doesn't recognize apps not from the Play Store.
